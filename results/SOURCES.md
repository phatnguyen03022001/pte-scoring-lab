# Sources and scope

`gopt_speechocean762.json` summarizes aggregate GOPT results on SpeechOcean762 held-out files using the authors' precomputed GOP features. SpeechOcean762 is attributed to OpenSLR SLR101 and licensed under CC BY 4.0: <https://www.openslr.org/101/>. The evaluated GOPT source is pinned to [`YuanGongND/gopt@bed909daf8eca035095871e51642525acc5b9b55`](https://github.com/YuanGongND/gopt/tree/bed909daf8eca035095871e51642525acc5b9b55).

The report uses the dataset's 0–10 labels and is research-only. The authors' checkpoint selection used test-set metrics, so these results are selection-exposed and not independent validation. They do not establish Pearson PTE validity or end-to-end performance on new audio. Correlations in the report describe SpeechOcean762 only.
